# Decisions — Paderborn bearing data (raw)

Dataset-specific decisions for the raw Paderborn bearing dataset (KAt DataCenter, Lessmeier et al., 2016). Index: `../../DECISIONS.md`.

## 2026-10-08 — D47: Download all 32 Paderborn bearing archives
**Decision:** All 32 archives (6 healthy, 12 artificially damaged, 14 really damaged bearings; one `.rar` per bearing, about 160 MB each, about 5.1 GB in total) plus `readme_versions.txt` are downloaded unchanged from https://groups.uni-paderborn.de/kat/BearingDataCenter/ into `data/raw/paderborn/`. Licence CC BY-NC 4.0; the source must be cited (Lessmeier et al., 2016, PHM Europe; KAt-DataCenter, Paderborn University). Conversion is decided separately.
**Alternatives considered:** Only the bearings needed for a first experiment (splits by bearing need many bearings; the full set is small).
**Rationale:** User's decision (2026-10-08). Step 2 of D35 (TF-C on the full Paderborn data, splits by bearing).

## 2026-10-08 — D48: Paderborn signals to convert: vibration and both motor currents
**Decision:** When the data are converted, the housing vibration and the two motor phase currents (all 64 kHz) are converted. Speed, torque, radial force and temperature (lower rates) are not converted for now. The conversion itself (mapping onto D1, metadata, timing) is decided separately.
**Alternatives considered:** Vibration only (the signal FD-A/FD-B use, according to the TF-C paper). All signals as recorded.
**Rationale:** User's decision (2026-10-08).

## 2026-11-04 — D212: Paderborn conversion (item 8 superseded by D213)
**Decision:** Where the literature has a standard, it is followed; where it has none (how the files are stored), the harness's conventions are (D1–D6, D139).
1. *Signals (literature):* vibration and both motor phase currents at 64 kHz, as D48. Lessmeier et al. (2016, PHM Europe) use vibration and motor current; most later work, including the Zhao et al. (2020) benchmark (D167), uses vibration only. Speed, torque, radial force (4 kHz) and temperature (1 Hz) are not converted (D48); the literature uses the nominal settings.
2. *Hierarchy (harness convention):* one run per bearing and operating condition (32 × 4 = 128 runs; run ID e.g. `K001_N15_M07_F10`), its 20 measurements of about 4 s as 20 captures (`m01`–`m20`), one stream (the 64 kHz channels share a clock), channels `vibration_1`, `phase_current_1`, `phase_current_2` (names as in the files).
3. *Operating condition:* the code from the file name per capture; nominal speed, load torque and radial force in the telemetry table (N15/N09 = 1500/900 rpm, M07/M01 = 0.7/0.1 Nm, F10/F04 = 1000/400 N; measuring logs and Lessmeier et al., 2016).
4. *Bearing labels (per-capture columns, D31):* transcribed from each bearing's damage sheet (`<code>.pdf` in its archive), cross-checked with Lessmeier et al. (2016) Tables 4, 5 and 7: group (healthy, artificial, real), damaged rings, number of damages, symptom, combination (S/R/M), arrangement, extent of damage, characteristic, damage method (one value per damage, in sheet order), manufacturer, run-in time (healthy) or lifetime in the accelerated test (real). Plus Lessmeier et al.'s class (healthy / OR / IR): KB23 and KB24 IR, KB27 OR (their Section 7.4, dominant damage); KI04 and KI14 IR although their sheets also list an outer-ring indentation (their Section 7.3). Sheet quirks recorded, not corrected: KA08's component is given as "AR" (Außenring, outer ring; OR in the paper); KI01's damage method is "n/a" (EDM in the paper).
5. *Units:* `unknown` (not stated in the files); the measuring logs' sensor details are kept in the run's extras (vibration: PCB 336C04, 100 mV/g, Kistler 5015 charge amplifier, 30 kHz filter; currents: LEM CKSR 15-NP).
6. *Status and type:* `final` (public benchmark); values as 64-bit floats as in the files (D6).
7. *Quirks:* the conversion checks every file and records, without fixing: unexpected variables (error), channels of unequal length, captures of unusual length, and measurements that are exact copies of another (KA04's measuring log says `N09_M07_F10_KA04_17.mat` was replaced by a copy of `_18`). Checked 2026-11-04: all 32 archives hold exactly 80 measurement files, numbered 1–20 per condition; KI04's faulty recordings (vibration sensor off, per its log) are not in the archive. A file that cannot be read is left out and recorded in the run's conversion notes (added 2026-11-04, Claude's choice in line with this item): `KA08/N15_M01_F10_KA08_2.mat` fails to parse (scipy: "Expecting matrix here"), so run `KA08_N15_M01_F10` has 19 captures.
8. *Reading (harness convention):* the archives stay packed (D47); the conversion reads them with the `unrar-cffi` package (Apache-2.0; added to the project's dependencies).
For the later task and split decisions, the standard protocols are: Lessmeier et al. (2016) Section 7.2 (train on artificial, test on real damage; 3 classes), Section 7.3 (real damage only: K001–K005, KA04/15/16/22/30, KI04/14/16/18/21; 3 bearings per class for training, 2 for test, 10 combinations), Section 7.4 (adds KB23/KB24 as IR, KB27 as OR); Zhao et al. (2020) benchmark (`CNN_Datasets/R_A/datasets/PU.py`): 13 real-damage bearings (KA04, KA15, KA16, KA22, KA30, KB23, KB24, KB27, KI14, KI16, KI17, KI18, KI21) as 13 classes, condition N15_M07_F10, measurement 1 only, vibration, 1,024-sample windows without overlap.
**Alternatives considered:** One run per measurement file (2,560 runs) or per bearing (32 runs). Converting the 4 kHz and 1 Hz signals. Unpacking the archives (about 18 GB).
**Rationale:** User's decision (2026-11-04): follow the literature where it has a standard. Items 2 and 8 have no literature standard and follow the harness's conventions (Claude's choice): a run is one test (one bearing at one setting, as CWRU's one bearing pair at one load, D139), a capture one uninterrupted acquisition (D1).

*Note (2026-11-04): conversion done (`scripts/convert_paderborn.py`): 128 runs, 2,559 captures (KA08_N15_M01_F10 has 19), 5.5 GB. Exact copies found, recorded in the captures column `identical_to`: KA04_N09_M07_F10 m18 = m17 (the copy the measuring log describes) and, not mentioned in the log, KA04_N15_M07_F04 m15 = m06, m19 = m05, m20 = m06. Measurement lengths 249,940–299,038 samples (median 256,001), stored as recorded. Values checked identical to the source files on spot checks.*

*Note (2026-11-04): item 8 does not work on the project environment. `unrar-cffi` (last release 2021) has Windows packages only for Python 3.7–3.9; on the project's Python 3.14 `uv sync` tries to build it and fails. It was removed from the dependencies again; the conversion above was run on the Linux side with a separate environment. How the archives are read is open (unpack once with 7-Zip, or `rarfile` with 7-Zip as its back end).*
