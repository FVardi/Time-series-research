# Decisions — University of Ottawa UODS-VAFDC

Dataset-specific decisions for the University of Ottawa ball-bearing vibration and acoustic fault data under constant load and speed (Sehri, Dumond & Bouchard, 2023, Data in Brief 49, 109327, doi 10.1016/j.dib.2023.109327; Mendeley Data y2px5tg92h; CC BY 4.0; also called UORED-VAFCLS). Adopted by D189, downloaded by D194. Index: `../../DECISIONS.md`.

## 2026-11-10 — D249: Ottawa conversion: one run per bearing, three stages as captures
**Decision:** The data note is followed where it says something, otherwise the harness conventions (D1–D6, D31). Code: `harness/data/ottawa_vafdc.py`, `scripts/convert_ottawa_vafdc.py`; test `tests/test_ottawa_vafdc.py`.
1. *Hierarchy:* one run per bearing (20 runs, `bearing_01`…`bearing_20`; one run-to-failure test, D1); its files H_k_0, X_k_1, X_k_2 are captures `stage_0`, `stage_1`, `stage_2` (healthy, fault developing, faulty). That H_k_0 is bearing k's healthy stage follows the data note's naming (first number = bearing ID); the speeds recorded in the files agree (e.g. H_6_0, O_6_1, O_6_2: 1,818 rpm).
2. *Signals:* one stream (NI USB-6212), two channels from the files' columns 1 and 2: `accelerometer` (m/s², PCB 623C01 on the drive-end bearing inside the motor casing) and `microphone` (V, PCB 130F20 within 2 cm of the bearing); 42 kHz, 420,000 samples (10 s). 64-bit floats as recorded (scale 1, offset 0): neither channel lies on an exact ADC grid (D6).
3. *Telemetry:* speed (rpm) and load (N) are the single non-zero values in the first row of columns 3 and 4 (zero elsewhere, checked); stored per capture as recorded. The data note's fifth column (temperature) is not in the files.
4. *Per-capture columns (D31):* condition letter (H, I, O, B, C), stage and its name, bearing ID, the bearing's fault (I 1–5, O 6–10, B 11–15, C 16–20), bearing model (NSK 6203ZZ for 1–5, FAFNIR 203KD for 6–20), `identical_to`. No start times (none in the files).
5. *Quirks (full scan of the 60 files, 2026-11-10):* **C_19_1 and C_19_2 are identical** (recorded as `identical_to`, not fixed); ball-fault files have no load (0 N), as the data note says, while their healthy files H_11_0…H_15_0 show 400 N; speeds 1,700–1,948 rpm against 1,750 nominal; no NaNs, no clipping (no flat tops). All 60 files 420,000 × 4, one variable named as the file.
6. *Status and source:* `final`; D24 per run (three file names, total size, latest modification time). Conversion checked on all 60 files: every sample of both channels equal to the source.
**Alternatives considered:** One run per file (60 runs; loses that the three stages are one test). Storing columns 3 and 4 as signals. Codes (not exact for these channels).
**Rationale:** User's decision (2026-11-10): approved Claude's proposals as given ("go ahead").

## 2026-11-10 — D250: Ottawa task and split: the dataset authors' protocol (Sehri et al., 2025) (details open)
**Decision:** Under D216 (dataset authors' protocol before other protocols), the split is that of Sehri, Dumond et al. (2025), "Integrating Spatial and Temporal Features for Bearing Fault Diagnosis: A Cross-Domain Analysis Using Vibration and Acoustic Data", International Journal of PHM (Table 3): ten domains of five files each (one per class H, I, O, B, C, from different bearings); odd domains hold the developing stage, even domains the faulty stage; domains 2, 4, 6, 8 for training and validation, domain 10 (H-10-0, I-5-2, O-10-2, B-15-2, C-20-2) for testing; five classes; input length 1,024 samples in the paper. No test bearing appears in the training domains.
Open (to be settled when the task is configured): how domains 2, 4, 6, 8 are divided into training and validation (not stated); window hop and normalisation for the harness (the paper's are not stated; it uses RMS preprocessing for its own model); whether the odd (developing) domains are used for pretraining; vibration, acoustic or both as inputs (the paper reports each).
**Alternatives considered:** Rosa/Vieira et al. (2025, arXiv 2509.22267; code github.com/gama-ufsc/bearing-data-leakage): bearing-wise, 3 of 5 bearings per fault mode for training, 100 random splits, multi-label (one split per dataset, D214, rules out 100 splits).
**Rationale:** User's decision (2026-11-10): approved Claude's proposals as given ("go ahead").
