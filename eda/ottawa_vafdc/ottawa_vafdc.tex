\begin{itemize}
  \item Each bearing has three recordings: stage 0 healthy, 1 fault developing, 2 faulty;
        60 recordings. The healthy class is stage 0 of every bearing.
  \item Stages chosen from 50 files per bearing: healthy 1--5, developing 25--30,
        faulty 45--50 (data note).
  \item One recording: 10\,s at 42\,kHz (420,000 samples), two channels (accelerometer,
        microphone).
  \item Speed 1700--1948\,rpm against 1750 nominal; it differs between bearings.
  \item Confounds: all inner-race bearings are NSK, all others FAFNIR; ball-fault stages 1--2
        have no load.
  \item C\_19\_1 and C\_19\_2 are identical (D249).
  \item Source: \texttt{FAULT\_OF\_BEARING}, \texttt{BEARING\_MODEL} in
        \texttt{harness/data/ottawa\_vafdc.py}; quirks D249.
\end{itemize}
