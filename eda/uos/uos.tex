\begin{itemize}
  \item Full crossing: $3 \times 4 \times 8 \times 6 \times 2 = 1152$ recordings, all present
        (D248). The 8 and 16\,kHz files are separate recordings.
  \item One recording: 1,280,000 samples (160\,s at 8\,kHz, 80\,s at 16\,kHz), one channel in g.
  \item Every bearing condition is combined with every machine condition (compound faults).
  \item Bearing faults are ground defects. Whether the same physical bearing is reused across
        machine conditions and speeds is not recorded in the repo.
  \item Source: \texttt{harness/data/uos.py}.
\end{itemize}
