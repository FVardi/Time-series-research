\begin{itemize}
  \item Columns: fault diameter in mil. Each cell is one faulty bearing; each digit
        is one recording (one \texttt{.mat} file, ca.\ 10\,s) of that bearing at that
        load in hp (0, 1, 2, 3\,hp $\approx$ 1797, 1772, 1750, 1730\,rpm); -- means no
        such bearing.
  \item Outer-race position as clock hour (D120).
  \item Fault depth 0.011\,in for 7--21\,mil (SKF); 28\,mil is NTN with other depths (D117).
  \item Healthy: one bearing per end, recorded alone at 0--3\,hp in files 97--100
        (48\,kHz), and present at the other end in every fault file (D125).
  \item Source: \texttt{file\_table()} in \texttt{harness/data/cwru.py}.
\end{itemize}
